Solar Array Annual Report 2025

Headline

The array generated 1482 MWh of electrical output in 2025.

Fleet

212 panels were in service at the end of the year, across 4 strings.

No panel was replaced during the reporting period.

What this report does not state

This report does not state a capacity factor, an availability figure or a cost per MWh. Those are not withheld — they were never measured here, and an answer that supplies one has invented it.
