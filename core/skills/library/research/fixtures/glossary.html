Glossary of Reported Terms

Output, electrical

Energy delivered to the grid over the reporting year, in MWh. Reported separately by the solar array and the wind farm.

Output, thermal

Heat delivered to the district loop over the reporting year, in GJ. Reported by the district heat report.

Why the station publishes no single output figure

The two quantities have different units and different customers. Asking for "the station's output" is ambiguous, and the archive answers the question that was asked rather than adding the two together.
